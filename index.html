About me

Curious by nature. Driven to create useful solutions.

My journey began with curiosity about how things work and a determination to create opportunities through learning. Today, I’m developing my path across computer science, technology projects, entrepreneurship and youth leadership.

I study Computer Science at the University of the People and enjoy collaborating with people who are building practical solutions. My interests include accessible technology, education, African innovation and solutions that respond to real community needs.

I am continuing my development through the Aspire Leaders Program and Aspire Institute’s Leadership Accelerator Powered by AI.
