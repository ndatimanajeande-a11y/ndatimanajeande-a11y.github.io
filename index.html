About me

Curious by nature. Driven to create useful solutions.

My journey is shaped by curiosity, persistence and a belief that learning should lead to useful action. I am building a path at the intersection of computer science, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, entrepreneurship and youth leadership.

As a Computer Science student at the University of the People, I look for opportunities to learn by doing: collaborating on technology projects, exploring new ideas and contributing to initiatives that respond to real needs. I have taken part in the Aspire Leaders Program and the Leadership Accelerator Powered by AI, and I serve in the Aspire community as a Campus Ambassador Leader.

My experiences have also included mentoring and supporting other learners, public speaking, debate and collaborative community initiatives. I want to keep growing as a responsible builder who uses technology to expand opportunity and create positive impact in Rwanda and across Africa.
