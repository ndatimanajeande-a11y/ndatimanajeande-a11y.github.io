## About this edition

This is a work in progress, not yet the completed 50-page book. Further chapters will develop the story of childhood and family, AgriTrust Rwanda, technology and artificial intelligence, leadership, community impact, and future ambitions.

Personal admissions and scholarship details are presented as author-provided information unless explicitly described as confirmed by correspondence. Pending decisions, administrative issues and financial barriers are not presented as completed achievements.
