Built for Kigali Commerce

Why Market Vendors & Shop Owners Switch to Ripple

Market vendors and small businesses can face difficulty arranging predictable, affordable deliveries. Ripple explores whether scheduled, consolidated routes could help address that challenge.

Proposed Price Before Pickup

The concept aims to offer clear RWF pricing by parcel size. Any savings compared with other transport options still need to be measured in a pilot.

3 Proposed Daily Runs

The proposed schedule is 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, and 4:00 PM from designated market hubs such as Kimironko Gate (KG 145 St). Actual operating days and departures must be confirmed.

Proposed QR Handover Workflow

The proposed workflow would assign a waybill reference at pickup and record handover at delivery. QR scanning is not implemented in this prototype.

Book Your Way

The concept is designed for lightweight access. WhatsApp is the only contact channel linked on this prototype; SMS and USSD are not connected.
